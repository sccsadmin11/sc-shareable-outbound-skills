# Ready-to-buy sample page

Give the prospect real, usable work before asking for anything: 5 accounts that fit THEIR ICP, each with a fresh public signal, 2 verified contacts and an email their rep could send today. One page per prospect COMPANY (address everyone you emailed there). Short and sweet: 3 cards only.

Reference example: https://gamma.app/docs/5-ready-to-buy-utlities-for-120Water-vr13pexnhk6cun6

## Inputs
- Prospect company, website, and the people you already emailed there (first names, what Email 1 was about).
- Sender details: name, title, company, website, email, call booking link (with a tracking tag).
- A logo image URL for the prospect.

## 1. Learn what the prospect sells and to whom
- Read their homepage, product/solution pages and customer stories.
- Write down: ICP company type (plural, e.g. "water utilities"), buyer titles, and the pain they solve.
- Build an exclusion list: logo wall, customer stories, press releases ("wins contract with..."), plus every account already named in your Email 1. Never suggest an existing customer.

## 2. Find 5 ready-to-buy accounts
- Each account must fit the prospect's ICP AND show a public signal from the last 60 days that makes now the right time (RFP/bid, funding or grant, new leader, expansion, regulation deadline, hiring push, etc.).
- Keep the source URL for every signal.
- Use 2-3 signal types across the 5 accounts; you'll name them in the intro and on the scale card.

## 3. Find 2 contacts per account and verify every email
- Contacts must match the prospect's buyer titles and be current in role.
- Email waterfall: people/company database first, then email finders. Verify every address; send catch-all/unknown results to a second verifier.
- Test whether the domain accepts any address before spending on people there. If the domain is catch-all and can't be verified, swap the account.
- Links: LinkedIn profile only. If no LinkedIn, show no link (never a news article).

## 4. Write one suggested email per account (to the stronger contact)
- Written as the prospect's rep, signed "{Your name}". Use "Hi {First},". Never "What's up" (that is only the sender's own cold-email opener).
- One short paragraph each:
  1. "Saw {account} {signal in plain words}." No calendar dates; say "just", "last month", etc.
  2. The pain that signal creates (why now). Plain words, no jargon.
  3. How the prospect's product fixes that pain. Only claims their site supports.
  4. One soft ask: "Worth a quick look?" / "Open to a quick look?"
  5. {Your name}
- 50-80 words. One ask only.

## 5. Build the Gamma page (3 cards, fluid, same theme every time)
**Card 1 (cover)**
- Prospect logo at top.
- H1: "5 ready-to-buy {ICP plural} for {Prospect}"
- "Made by {Sender company}" with the sender website hyperlinked.

**Card 2: "Whom to reach and why now?"**
- "Hey {First1} and {First2},"
- "I emailed you both last week about {Email 1 topic}. Instead of sending another "just following up," I figured I'd show you what we'd actually do."
- "I found 5 {ICP plural} that {signal 1}, {signal 2} or {signal 3} in the last month. Then I found 2 people at each one and verified every email."
- 5 collapsible sections (click to open), stacked one per row, left-aligned:
  - Visible: **{Account, location}** + "**Why now:** {one line that leads with the pain}".
  - Inside: "Contacts" (bold name, title, mailto email, LinkedIn link if any), then "Suggested email" with each paragraph separated, no indent.

**Card 3: "How to Scale this?"**
- "For {Prospect}, we can run this at scale by:"
  - Mapping your entire total addressable market (TAM) of {ICP plural}
  - Checking it against every possible signal ({signal 1}, {signal 2}, etc.)
  - Finding people at those companies and reaching out to them cold via email and LinkedIn
- "If you want to learn more about this, here's my call booking link:" then the booking page EMBEDDED (not just a link).
- "Thank you," / sender first name / title / company (website hyperlinked) / email.

## 6. QA before sharing
- No leftover placeholders ({ICP...}, {Company}) and no typos in the title.
- All 10 emails verified, none catch-all; LinkedIn-only links.
- No existing customers; no accounts reused from Email 1.
- Every signal within 60 days and sourced.
- Public view works in a private browser window.

## 7. Follow-up email that carries the link (draft only; the sender reviews and sends)
Reply in the original thread, one per person:
"Hi {First}, rather than bug you with another follow-up, I put together 5 {ICP plural} your team could reach this week, with 10 verified contacts and the email I'd send them: {page link}. Are you open to discussing this further?" plus the sender's signature and opt-out line.
